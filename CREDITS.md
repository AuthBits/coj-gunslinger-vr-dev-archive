# Credits

Everyone whose work, research, or game this project builds on or references.
Policy: **get credited, or ask us to stop** — if you're named here and want
your name or information changed or removed, open an issue (or use the
contact in `CONTRIBUTING.md`) and tell us exactly what to remove; we'll do it
promptly, no questions asked.

## The game & its makers

- **Techland S.A.** (PL) — developer of Call of Juarez: Gunslinger and of the
  Chrome Engine. This project mods their game; the engine and all game files
  are theirs.
- **Ubisoft** — publisher (2013).

## Method & tooling

- **TefMeister** — [flat-to-vr-RE-toolkit](https://github.com/TefMeister/flat-to-vr-RE-toolkit)
  (the PLAYBOOK.md in this repo is copied verbatim from it, CC BY 4.0), and
  [flat-to-vr-cross-engine-research](https://github.com/TefMeister/flat-to-vr-cross-engine-research)
  (public flat→VR knowledge library). Their per-game five/six-repo convention
  is the structure of this project.
- **Valve** — OpenVR / SteamVR (submission runtime, reference).
- **The Khronos Group** — OpenXR (portable alternative runtime, reference).
- **praydog** — UEVR: studied as the reference implementation for per-eye
  matrix construction, OpenXR/OpenVR submission, HMD pose, and frame timing
  (techniques studied, nothing copied).
- **TsudaKageyu & contributors** — MinHook (planned hooking library).
- **mrexodia, Sigma, torusrxxx & the x64dbg community** — x64dbg (planned
  debugger).
- **dariushoule** — x64dbg-automate + x64dbg-skills (planned debugger
  automation bridge).
- **Animation Labs (vorpX)** — prior existence of a Z3D/cinema profile for
  this game is cited as third-party evidence that its D3D9 renderer is
  hookable. No vorpX code, binaries, or profiles are used, inspected, or
  copied.
